04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Small print, plain English.

How long does the ad stay on the laptop?+

Each $50 booking keeps one approved sticker in your selected position for 50 days—equivalent to $1 per day.

What artwork should I send?+

A high-resolution PNG is ideal. Simple logos with good contrast work best at sticker size. Final size is confirmed before printing.

What proof will I receive?+

You’ll receive a clear, timestamped photo of the sticker applied in your selected position, sent to the email used for booking.

Can a campaign be rejected?+

Yes. Illegal, hateful, misleading, adult, political, gambling, or unsafe promotions are not accepted. If artwork is declined, payment is refunded.
